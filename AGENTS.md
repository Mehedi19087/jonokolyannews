## Media Specifications

- **Article Image Standard**: Every article image size is fixed to 1200x650 (`aspect-[1200/650]`).
- HTML attributes: `width={1200}` and `height={650}` with `max-w-full`.
- OpenGraph metadata: `og:image:width` is 1200, and `og:image:height` is 650.
